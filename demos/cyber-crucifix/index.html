Cyber Crucifix

Psychedelic 3D WebGL — chroma melt · chroma frame · rgb splits · random strobe

A cyber crucifix floating in a field of sixteen thousand swaying grass blades. Circuit-etched beams carry traveling light pulses; a hologram wireframe spins around them; shockwave rings roll out across the ground. The post-processing pass does the rest: radial chromatic aberration, glitch slice jumps, chroma melt (per-channel vertical smear), a chroma frame with RGB-fringed borders, and random strobe bursts. Built as a trial by DeepSeek v4 Flash 0731 — drag to orbit, scroll to zoom.
